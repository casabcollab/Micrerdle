Micrerdle is made by a team of med students. It's primarily a fun, Step 1–relevant study tool, although it can be used by students and providers in any field! The cases, hints and music were made by our team. Some of the art is original, and some is made with the help of generative AI.

However, we're interested in becoming fully human-made! If you have an idea for a case, or like to make art, email us your 8-bit style artwork at casabcollab@gmail.com. Any and all skill levels are welcome, and you'll receive credit for it as well!

Also, please email us if we've gotten anything wrong.
